# 🚨 Emergency Call-Centre Intake Script (5W Framework)

## 📞 Call-Taker Opening

**Operator:**
> Emergency Services. What is the address of the emergency?

---

## 1. 📍 Location — *Where is the emergency?*

**Operator:**
> Please tell me the exact location of the incident.
> Can you confirm the street name and unit or house number?
> What floor or room is this on?
> Are there any nearby landmarks that can help responders find you quickly?

---

## 2. ⚠️ Situation — *What has happened?*

**Operator:**
> Please describe what has happened.
> Is this a fire, an accident, or a medical emergency?
> Is anyone unconscious or seriously injured?
> Are there any immediate dangers such as smoke or hazardous conditions?

---

## 3. ☎️ Caller Identification — *Who is calling?*

**Operator:**
> May I have your name?
> What is the best phone number to reach you in case we get disconnected?

---

## 4. 👥 Casualties — *How many people are affected?*

**Operator:**
> How many people are involved?
> Can you describe their condition?
> Is anyone unconscious, trapped, or bleeding?

---

## 5. ⏱️ Stay on the Line — *Wait for further questions*

**Operator:**
> Please stay on the line while I gather more information.
> Do not hang up until I tell you it is okay to do so.

---

✅ Continue asking follow-up questions as required until all necessary information for dispatch has been collected.
